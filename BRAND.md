# DIGITS brand kit

The DIGITS identity combines an open grid with a final plus sign: structured data with room to connect, extend, and participate. Use the supplied assets directly rather than redrawing the mark.

## Verbal identity

**Primary tagline:** Wearable data that works together.

**Supporting line:** Practical interoperability for sensor data, research, and AI.

**Social bio:** An NIH-funded consortium making wearable and sensor data work across devices, platforms, and studies—for research and responsible AI.

**Long description:** DIGITS brings researchers, clinicians, standards experts, and technology partners together to connect existing standards with practical profiles, mappings, adapters, and validation tools for wearable and sensor data.

Recommended account presentation:

- Display name: `DIGITS`
- Account handle when `DIGITS` is unavailable: `OpenDIGITS`
- Avoid using `OpenDIGITS` as the initiative name in headlines; it is the account handle.

## Asset selection

| Use                                              | Preferred file                            | Dimensions  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Social profile image                             | `public/brand/digits-avatar-dark.png`     | 1024 × 1024 |
| Alternate light profile image                    | `public/brand/digits-avatar-light.png`    | 1024 × 1024 |
| Website, document, or email on light backgrounds | `public/brand/digits-lockup-on-light.svg` | 1600 × 320  |
| Slides or graphics on dark backgrounds           | `public/brand/digits-lockup-on-dark.svg`  | 1600 × 320  |
| Open Graph and general social sharing            | `public/brand/digits-social-card.png`     | 1200 × 630  |
| Bluesky banner                                   | `public/brand/digits-bluesky-banner.png`  | 1500 × 500  |
| LinkedIn cover                                   | `public/brand/digits-linkedin-cover.png`  | 1128 × 191  |

SVG files are preferred for documents and presentation software that supports them. PNG exports are provided for social platforms and tools that rasterize SVG inconsistently.

## Usage

- Preserve the mark’s proportions and keep it upright.
- Leave clear space around the mark equal to at least one grid cell.
- Use the on-light lockup on white or warm-neutral surfaces and the on-dark lockup on dark slate surfaces.
- Keep the full expansion of DIGITS in formal materials when space permits.
- Do not place the mark over visually busy photography or recolor individual cells.
- The tagline may appear without quotation marks and should retain the final period in display applications.

The palette is centralized in `src/styles/tokens.css`. The downloadable files are generated by `scripts/generate-brand-assets.mjs`; run `npm run brand:generate` after changing brand copy or colors.
